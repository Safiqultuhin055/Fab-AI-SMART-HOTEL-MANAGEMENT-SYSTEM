{% load i18n %}{% comment %} The default receptionist: original artwork, drawn here rather than shipped as a photograph. Why it is illustrated and not a real face. A stock portrait means putting a person who does not exist in front of guests as though they were staff, using a likeness nobody licensed to us. A hotel that wants a real photograph uploads one (Settings -> AI, "kiosk avatar") and it replaces this. Their image, their consent, their brand. Why it moves. A still figure on a lobby screen reads as a poster, and people do not talk to posters. The motion is small on purpose — breathing, blinking, a slight sway — because a lobby panel that animates hard is exhausting to stand in front of. The mouth only moves while an answer is actually being spoken, so the movement is information rather than decoration. Every animated part is driven from CSS keyed on the state machine (#kiosk-avatar[data-state]), so nothing here needs JavaScript and it all stops under prefers-reduced-motion. {% endcomment %}

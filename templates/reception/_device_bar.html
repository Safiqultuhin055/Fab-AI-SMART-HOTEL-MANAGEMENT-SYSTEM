A lobby kiosk has a fixed rig — a webcam on the stand, a boundary mic, a powered speaker — plus the machine's own built-in versions that nobody wants. The browser picks "default", which is regularly the wrong one. Staff set this once at install; the choice is stored per property and every guest afterwards gets the right devices. One partial, two homes: the terminal puts it in the page header beside the clock, the staff console keeps it in the widget's own header. The ids are what kiosk-devices.js binds to, so it must appear exactly once per page. The caveats live in the bar's own tooltip, with #device-note carrying the same text for a screen reader. Three lines of small print across the top of a lobby screen is noise; a guest never needs to read it, and the staff member setting the terminal up will hover. Hidden entirely where the browser cannot enumerate devices. {% endcomment %}

{% comment %} No kiosk-devices.js: the camera/microphone/speaker pickers are a lobby rig's control. Staff pick the webcam on the stand and the powered speaker once at install, because the browser's "default" is regularly the wrong one on a machine with four audio devices in it. A guest on their own phone or laptop has one microphone and has already chosen it — three dropdowns asking them to re-pick their own hardware is a setup screen in front of a booking, and the script that fills them had nothing to bind to here anyway. kiosk.js copes with the bar being absent by design (see `devices()`): the microphone opens on the browser's default device and audio plays out of the browser's default output, which on a personal device is the right answer. No kiosk-enrol.js either: face capture is a lobby flow, and a web page asking to photograph somebody at home is not a flow anybody designed. {% endcomment %} {% endif %}
